The gap

A pipeline run on chance

The only missing piece is the structure to connect them on a schedule.

05

Why this shape works

The structure is adapted from the Albers Mentor Program.

i

Small groups

Peer support, a lighter load on each mentor, and cross-campus networks that outlast the year.

ii

Meet before you match

Letting both sides feel each other out first is what makes matches stick.

iii

Cadence beats intensity

Reliable monthly meetings outperform an intense start that fizzles by winter.

iv

Something to do together

Site visits and shared NABA events give the relationship a purpose beyond coffee chats.
